Mojo font usage
Mojo Pixel Serif Draft v0.200: Wordmark and main display headings. Supplied TTF.
Mojo Serif Refined v0.601: Subtitles and editorial section headings. Supplied TTF.
Nunito Sans variable regular v3.101: Body text (Regular), controls (Medium), emphasis (Semibold). Supplied TTF.
Nunito Sans variable italic v3.101: Italic body text. Supplied TTF.
Custom Mojo licence documents and WOFF/WOFF2 files have not been supplied. Nunito Sans SIL OFL licence and self-hosted WOFF2 files are included.
