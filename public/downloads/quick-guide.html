Main logo

Approved

Keep the supplied cat to the left of lowercase mojo in Mojo Draft. Use the dark logo on a light background. Do not stretch, rotate, or rearrange it. Keep text and images away from the logo. Approved minimum sizes and spacing measurements are not defined.
